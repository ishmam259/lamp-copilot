# Official website and launch announcement: what they add to the PDFs

Captured 18 Oct 2026 from https://fintechathon.g-ican.com/#/en (International Track view) and the launch story it links to on the IEEE CIS Shenzhen site. Quotes are verbatim.

## International Track challenge brief (website, "Challenge Brief" pop-up; this is its full text)

> **AI as a Financial Participant**
> Select one direction and deliver a working financial Agent that demonstrates intent understanding, task planning, safe execution, user control and measurable value in a financial scenario.

## Topic E as worded on the website (different from the PDFs)

> **E — Small-business Finance Assistant:** Cash flow, reconciliation and operational recommendations

The PDFs say: "A finance copilot for small-business owners, offering financing-option matching, cash-flow management with alerts, and business financial analysis."
The IEEE launch story says: "Topic E – SME Finance Copilot: Financing and cash-flow support for small businesses."

Intro line on the website: "All directions address the theme 'AI as a Financial Participant'. Teams select one direction for in-depth implementation. Directions A–E are new or expanded this year; Direction F is the original Agent Wallet."

## Awards (website)

Each track (Domestic AI, International AI, Data Analytics) awards one First Prize (RMB 100,000), Second Prize (RMB 80,000), Third Prize (RMB 50,000) and **Best Creative Award** (RMB 20,000). The PDFs call it "Best Innovation Award" and add several Excellence Awards.

## Academic advisers (website)

Wang Shouyang (CAS), **Qiang Yang (Chief AI Adviser, WeBank)**, Li Wenrong (CityU), Li Jianqiang (Dean of AI, Shenzhen University), Chai Yunpeng (Renmin University), Dacheng Xiu (Chicago Booth, econometrics and finance), Wolfgang Karl Härdle (Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin, digital assets), Joshua Bongard (University of Vermont).

## Registration (IEEE launch story)

Download the registration form from the official website, complete it and send it to fintechathon@ican-x.com with the subject "International Track Registration – Country – Team Name – Team Leader's Full Name". The organiser confirms within 48 hours.
