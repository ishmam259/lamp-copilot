## Adding the voice

- Record the voice in one take while the video plays, for example in CapCut, Clipchamp (built into Windows) or DaVinci Resolve. Drop the MP4 on the timeline, record the voice over it, and export at 1080p.
- If a line runs long, slow that stretch of video slightly rather than rushing the line. The video must stay under 5:00; there are 11 seconds spare.
- Upload as unlisted to YouTube or Google Drive, put the link in `video/README.md`, and keep the MP4.
